The bean shelf

Know what you have, and what is ready to brew.

  • Rest, counted for you. Each bag shows the days since roast, and the days of rest it has left before it is at its best.
  • Open, resting, frozen, finished. Bags move along the shelf as you use them, and a freezer shelf keeps the ones you've put away, including whether you split them into doses.
  • Photos of the bag. Take or pick one, then trim and crop it to the label.
  • Tasting notes. Rate each brew and pick flavours from a tasting wheel laid out like the SCA's.
  • Search everything. One search across every bag and every brew.

Grind that follows the bean

Dialling in is the part worth remembering.

  • Your grinder's own dial. 198 grinders are drawn with their real collar and steps, including stepped, stepless and lettered dials. See them all on the Gear page.
  • A collar you can feel. Flick it and it coasts and settles; detents click with haptics that scale with speed.
  • Where it worked before. Marks on the dial show every setting already brewed on that bean, and the app starts from the last good one.
  • Per set of burrs. Swap burrs and each set keeps its own history.
  • The dial-in, drawn. A chart of where the collar has been across a bag's life, and how each brew was rated.

Recipes, timed

From pour-over to cold brew to milk drinks.

  • Scaled to your dose. Change the coffee and every pour and the water follow.
  • Step by step. Prep first, then a running clock walks you through each pour, with the pour curve drawn.
  • Your scale, live. With a Bluetooth scale connected, the weight shows right in the recipe.
  • Your own recipes. Write them, copy one and tweak it, and keep them by method.

Shots, graphed

For Gaggiuino and GaggiMate machines on your home Wi-Fi.

  • Shots arrive on their own. Pull a shot and it lands in the app with pressure, flow and weight graphed.
  • Read each phase. Preinfusion and extraction are labelled, with what ended each phase and what that says about the grind.
  • Compare two shots. One above the other, to see what changed.
  • Consistency. A score for how alike a shot is to the ones you pulled the same way.
  • Profiles. Keep your Gaggiuino profiles, and the community's, in one library.
  • Share a shot. Send its graph as a picture.

Import a coffee

Skip the typing.

  • From a link. Paste a roaster's product page and the bag fills in: name, roaster, origin, producer, process, varietal, elevation, notes and photo.
  • From the bag. Scan the QR code printed on it.
  • From a collection. Paste a roaster's "new arrivals" page and pick the coffee you bought.
  • Even after it sells out. If the page is gone, the app looks for a saved copy in the Internet Archive.
  • Almost any shop. It reads roasters' own sites whatever they are built on, in a dozen languages. See the 191 tested roasters on the Gear page.

Private, and synced

Your library is yours.

  • No account, no tracking. The developer never sees your data. See the privacy policy.
  • iCloud sync. Your iPhone and iPad stay in step through your own iCloud.
  • Back up anytime. Export the whole library to Files, and restore it from there.
  • Make it yours. Eighteen themes, from national parks to nixie tubes.
