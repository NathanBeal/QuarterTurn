Support

Quarter Turn

Quarter Turn is a coffee log: bags, brews, recipes and espresso shots, on your iPhone and iPad.

Contact

For questions, problems or suggestions, email quarterturn.support@gmail.com. Please include your device, its iOS version and the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings).

Common questions

My library isn't on my other device.
Syncing goes through iCloud. Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud is on for Quarter Turn (Settings > your name > iCloud). Changes can take a minute to arrive.

How do I back up my library?
In the app, open Settings and choose Export library to Files. To bring a backup back, choose Restore from a backup.

Which scales work with the app?
Bluetooth scales from Acaia, BOOKOO and Decent. Bluetooth must be allowed for Quarter Turn in Settings > Privacy & Security > Bluetooth.

The app can't find my espresso machine.
Gaggiuino and GaggiMate machines are reached over your home network. The phone needs to be on the same Wi-Fi network as the machine, and Local Network must be allowed for Quarter Turn in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network.

A roaster's link didn't fill in the bag.
The app reads the details from the roaster's product page, and not every shop's page can be read. Send the link to the address above and we'll take a look.
