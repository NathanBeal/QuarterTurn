Roaster imports

Paste a link to a coffee on a roaster's site, or scan the QR code on the bag, and the bag fills itself in: name, roaster, origin, process, varietal, elevation, tasting notes and photo.

Shopify roasters Most shops

Most specialty roasters sell on Shopify, and any Shopify product page can be read. A collection link works too: pick the coffee from the list.

Sold out?

If the roaster has taken a coffee's page down, the app looks for a saved copy in the Internet Archive instead.

Nothing matches that. Send it to us below and we'll look at adding it.

Missing something?

If your machine, grinder, scale or roaster isn't here, tell us what it is and we'll look at adding it. For a grinder, the dial's range and steps help.
