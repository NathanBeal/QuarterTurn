Privacy Policy

Quarter Turn - effective 2 October 2026

Quarter Turn is a coffee log. It is built so that your data stays yours: the developer does not collect, receive, store or sell any information about you.

What the app stores

The bags, brews, recipes, settings, photos and shots you record are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they are also synced through Apple's iCloud to your other devices, in your private iCloud database. The developer cannot see that database. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or logins.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDKs that do.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any server the developer runs.

Permissions and connections

  • Camera and Photos - only when you choose to take or pick a photo of a bag or your equipment. Photos stay on your device and in your iCloud.
  • Bluetooth - to connect to a coffee scale you pair. Weight and timer readings go from the scale to the app and nowhere else.
  • Local network - only if you set up a networked espresso machine (such as Gaggiuino or GaggiMate), to read shots from that machine on your own network.
  • Web pages you paste - if you paste a roaster's product link to fill in a bag, the app downloads that one page from the roaster's website to read the details. That website sees an ordinary page request.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its data from your device. To remove the synced copy, delete the app's data in your device's iCloud settings (Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage).

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
