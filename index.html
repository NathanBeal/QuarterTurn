A coffee log for the bags you buy and the brews you make, with your grind remembered for every bean.

Coming soon to the App Store

For iPhone and iPad

01

Every bag on the shelf

Track what is open, resting, frozen and finished, with each bag's rest counted from its roast date.

02

Grind that follows the bean

Dial in on a collar drawn like your grinder's, and start from the setting that worked last time.

03

Recipes that scale

Pour-over, iced, cold brew and espresso drinks, scaled to your dose and walked through against a timer.

04

Your scale, live

Read dose and yield straight off a BOOKOO scale while you weigh. Acaia and Decent are in beta.

05

Shots from your machine

On a Gaggiuino or GaggiMate, every shot arrives on its own, with pressure, flow and weight graphed. See all supported gear.

06

Yours, and only yours

No account and no tracking. Your library stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Paste a roaster's link or scan the bag's QR code, and the coffee fills itself in.
